Top Places to Visit

Three sites, one journey through the origins of Buddhism and higher learning.

UNESCO Site

Bodh Gaya

Where Gautama Buddha attained enlightenment under the Bodhi tree. One of the most sacred sites in the world.

Pilgrims and monks from across Asia gather at the Mahabodhi Temple year-round. The site draws visitors from Sri Lanka, Thailand, Japan, and beyond, each tradition adding its own monastery nearby.

UNESCO Site

Nalanda

Home to the world's first great university, dating back to the 5th century. A UNESCO World Heritage Site.

At its peak, Nalanda housed thousands of monks and students studying philosophy, medicine, and mathematics — a center of learning that drew scholars from as far as China and Central Asia.

Sacred Hills

Rajgir

Ancient city surrounded by hills, where Buddha preached his teachings. Famous for Vishwa Shanti Stupa and hot springs.

A ropeway ride takes visitors up to the Vishwa Shanti Stupa for panoramic views, while the natural hot springs at the base of the hills have drawn pilgrims for centuries.

Plan Your Visit

Best Time to Visit

October to March, when the weather is cool and comfortable for sightseeing.

How to Reach

Fly into Patna (Jay Prakash Narayan Airport), then travel onward by road or rail.

Getting Around

Bodh Gaya, Nalanda, and Rajgir are well connected by road, making a circuit easy.

Suggested Duration

3–4 days is enough to comfortably cover all three heritage sites.
